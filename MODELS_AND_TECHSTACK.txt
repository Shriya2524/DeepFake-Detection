MODEL 5: AI-Generated Image Detector (Multi-Check System) - NEW!
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Location: src/api/ai_generated/
Files:
  - image_classifier_model.keras  (Trained Keras CNN model)
  - multi_check_detector.py       (5-point analysis system)
  - __init__.py                   (Python module)

Purpose:
  - Detect AI-Generated images vs Natural/Real photos
  - Targets: DALL-E, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, and other AI generators

Architecture:
  5-Point Multi-Check Analysis System:
  
  +-----------------------+--------+----------------------------------------+
  | Check                 | Weight | Method                                 |
  +-----------------------+--------+----------------------------------------+
  | 1. Neural Network     | 30%    | Keras CNN trained on AI vs Real        |
  | 2. Frequency Analysis | 20%    | FFT-based pattern detection            |
  | 3. Noise Analysis     | 18%    | Variance of variance in noise          |
  | 4. Color Distribution | 17%    | LAB entropy + saturation variance      |
  | 5. Edge Analysis      | 15%    | Canny edge smoothness detection        |
  +-----------------------+--------+----------------------------------------+

CNN Model Details:
  - Framework: TensorFlow/Keras
  - Input Size: 128x128 pixels (RGB)
  - Output: [AI_probability, Natural_probability]
  - Architecture: Custom CNN classifier

Decision Logic:
  - Majority voting: 3+ checks = AI-Generated
  - Adaptive threshold based on consensus level
  - Confidence calculated from final weighted score

Confidence Levels:
  - "Very High (Unanimous)" - All 5 checks agree
  - "High (Majority Agreement)" - 4+ checks agree
  - "Medium" - 3/5 checks agree
  - "Low" - Less than 3/5 agreement
