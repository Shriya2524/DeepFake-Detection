================================================================================
                        WHAT IS ACCURACY?
================================================================================

In simple terms, ACCURACY tells us:

    "Out of 100 images/videos, how many did the model correctly identify 
     as REAL or FAKE?"

    Example: If accuracy is 95%, the model correctly identifies 95 out of 
             100 samples.

================================================================================
                     METRICS WE MEASURE
================================================================================

1. ACCURACY (Main Metric)
   -----------------------
   Formula: (Correct Predictions) / (Total Predictions) x 100
   
   Example: Model tested on 100 images
            - Correctly identified 95 images
            - Accuracy = 95/100 = 95%


2. PRECISION
   ----------
   "When the model says FAKE, how often is it actually fake?"
   
   Example: Model flagged 50 images as FAKE
            - 48 were actually fake
            - Precision = 48/50 = 96%


3. RECALL (Sensitivity)
   ---------------------
   "Out of all actual FAKE images, how many did we catch?"
   
   Example: Dataset has 60 fake images
            - Model detected 54 of them
            - Recall = 54/60 = 90%


4. F1 SCORE
   ---------
   "Balance between Precision and Recall"
   
   - Combines both metrics into one number
   - Higher is better (max = 1.0 or 100%)


5. ROC-AUC (Area Under Curve)
   --------------------------
   "How well can the model distinguish between REAL and FAKE?"
   
   - Score ranges from 0.5 (random guess) to 1.0 (perfect)
   - 0.98 means excellent discrimination ability


6. CONSENSUS (For Multi-Check Systems)
   ------------------------------------
   "How many analysis methods agree on the result?"
   
   - Used in AI-Generated Detection
   - Higher consensus = more confident result
   - 5/5 checks agree = Very High confidence

MODEL 5: AI-Generated Image Detector
------------------------------------
Purpose: Detect AI-Generated images (DALL-E, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, etc.)
Method: 5-Point Multi-Check Consensus Analysis

Individual Check Performance:
+------------------------+----------------+----------------------------------+
| Analysis Method        | Weight         | What It Detects                  |
+------------------------+----------------+----------------------------------+
| Neural Network (CNN)   | 30%            | Trained patterns in AI images    |
| Frequency Analysis     | 20%            | FFT patterns unique to AI        |
| Noise Pattern          | 18%            | Uniform noise (AI signature)     |
| Color Distribution     | 17%            | Unnatural color histograms       |
| Edge Characteristics   | 15%            | Smooth/artificial edges          |
+------------------------+----------------+----------------------------------+

Decision Logic:
+------------------+---------------------+------------------------------------+
| Consensus Level  | Adaptive Threshold  | Confidence Level                   |
+------------------+---------------------+------------------------------------+
| >= 80% (4-5/5)   | 0.45                | High - Very reliable result        |
| >= 60% (3/5)     | 0.50                | Medium - Reliable result           |
| < 60% (1-2/5)    | 0.55                | Low - Less certain                 |
+------------------+---------------------+------------------------------------+

Majority Voting Rules:
- 3+ checks vote AI --> Classified as AI-Generated
- 4+ checks vote Natural --> Classified as Natural/Real
- Unanimous (5/5) --> Very High confidence

Confidence Levels Explained:
- "Very High (Unanimous)" - All 5 checks agree
- "High (Majority Agreement)" - 4+ checks agree
- "High (Natural Majority)" - 4+ checks say Natural
- "Medium" - 3/5 checks agree
- "Low" - Less than 3/5 agreement
